How the musical key is chosen
Root note = (3R + 5G + 7B) mod 12 using the average RGB of the whole image.
Mode = major for brighter images, minor for darker images, unless you override it above.
Every scan-line note is quantised to the nearest note in that scale.

Voices are spread across registers: lower lines play lower notes and upper lines play higher notes. This keeps the result more musical while staying fully determined by the image.
